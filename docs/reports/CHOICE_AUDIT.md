# Choice audit

Generated by `scripts/choice_audit.py`: 3000 games between 120 random decks from the whole card pool, `simple_trade` on both seats. Regret: 2400 sampled real choices, 8 reshuffles per option to find the best alternative, then 32 fresh reshuffles for it vs the baseline (0 replays diverged and were dropped).

**gain** = win rate with the best alternative minus win rate with the baseline's pick, for the player making the choice (percentage points, ± standard error). **per game** = gain × how often the choice comes up: the win rate a player would gain per game if this choice were made well.

How to read it: both players are a deterministic heuristic, so near the end of a game a small change can flip the opponent's whole plan and the result with it. A large gain on a rare choice is often that, not a better pick. **baseline vs a random pick** is the steadier sign of whether a baseline rule is sensible.
